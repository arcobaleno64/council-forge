## Reasoning

TASK-1087 §Out of Scope forbids fixing old tasks' qualitative deltas here (that is the
follow-up per-batch governed work) and forbids mutating old-task history. The back-audit's
job is to REVEAL + immediately re-judge the highest-risk subset HONESTLY, then hand
remediation to dedicated tasks. Fixing old security code inside a read-only back-audit task
would itself violate the scope discipline the audit exists to enforce. The re-review is
honest BOTH ways: "robust at completion bar, no qualitative delta" was an allowed verdict;
the reviewers were explicitly told not to manufacture issues nor whitewash.
