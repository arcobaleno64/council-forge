## Enforcement Classification

### Why baseline snapshot was not modified

`artifacts/governance/verify-floor-baseline.v3.4.json` is a frozen snapshot created by TASK-1009 at commit `70bdd0e07125ebdb83ad3eb9adefd5e109eeaa29`.
